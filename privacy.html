Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Flight of the Bumblebee · B² Studio
Contact: b2studiosupport@gmail.com

Your progress stays on your device

The game stores earned nectar, upgrades, cosmetic ownership and equipment, medals, route unlocks, objectives, flight checkpoints, sound/camera/graphics preferences and optional ad-reward claim limits in its private local storage. Recovery copies and preserved unreadable saves stay there too. B² Studio does not operate game accounts or receive your saved game through the app.

Offline play and optional advertising

Normal gameplay, earned upgrades and cosmetics work offline and never require an ad or purchase. This version includes optional rewarded advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Successful Adventure missions, including tutorial flights and the finale, and eligible Endless results may offer 1,000 Nectar after completing an ad, limited to one bonus per flight and three per UTC day, with two minutes between Nectar bonuses. Failed Adventure missions may instead offer one optional continue per attempt with one heart, a countdown and immediate collision clearance. A full restart remains free; a continue does not add Nectar, remove hits or replenish power-ups. Expeditions do not offer ads. You can decline and continue playing with your normal rewards. Ads need an Internet connection. The testing APK uses Google's test advertising; live advertising requires a separately configured build.

Google advertising and privacy checks

Google's User Messaging Platform checks applicable privacy requirements at app launch. The Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized and an ad requested after you choose the optional reward. Google may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions, diagnostics and device/app identifiers to provide advertising, measure service performance and prevent fraud. Its network data is encrypted in transit. B² Studio does not add a separate analytics service or pass your save, email, name or custom player identifier to Google. Android Advertising ID and ad-services ID/attribution/topics permissions are removed. Removing those permissions does not mean that Google processes no other data.

Family audience and advertising choices

All ad requests are configured for child age treatment, a maximum G content rating and non-personalized advertising. The privacy SDK is also told to apply under-age-of-consent treatment to all users; the game does not ask for a birth date or age. Required privacy checks must permit an ad before it is loaded. If Google requires a privacy-options entry, Ad privacy choices appears under Credits → Privacy & support. You may always decline the optional ad and play offline. These controls do not operate a child account or social service.

Google's own data handling and retention are governed by https://policies.google.com/privacy . See its SDK disclosure at https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure . Google may retain service information independently; clearing this game's storage does not necessarily delete information previously processed by Google.

Backups you choose to export

Backup & restore uses the system file picker. Only the file you select is accessed; the game does not request access to all your files. An exported backup contains game progress, preferences and the optional reward ledger, not your email or account details. If you choose a cloud or document provider, that provider may store or transfer the file under its own privacy policy. B² Studio does not receive the backup. You control where it is kept and when it is deleted.

Permissions

Vibration provides gameplay feedback. Internet and network-state access support Google privacy checks and optional ads. This version does not request camera, microphone, contacts, GPS/location or Advertising ID access. Approximate location can still be inferred by Google from an Internet IP address.

Keeping and deleting your progress

Local data remains until you clear this app's storage or uninstall it. Android app backup is disabled for this version. Clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently remove earned progress and its recovery copies. The game does not provide cloud synchronization or a remote recovery service. Backups you explicitly export outside the app can remain after uninstalling. Restoring a selected backup replaces local progress after your confirmation; it does not merge saves. A private copy of the previous valid save is kept for undo, but it is removed with the app. Keep your exported file safe.

Support you choose to send

If you contact b2studiosupport@gmail.com outside the game, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your inquiry. Avoid sending information unnecessary for support. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence by contacting the same address; correspondence needed for an unresolved inquiry or applicable obligations may be retained. Your email provider processes messages under its own policy. A parent or guardian can contact support about a child's inquiry without providing unnecessary personal information.

Policy updates

Changes to advertising or other data services will be reflected in this policy and applicable privacy controls. This policy describes the optional rewarded-ad integration; no forced interstitial, paid unlock, game account, chat or social sharing service is provided.

About this website

This studio website uses no analytics, advertising scripts, contact forms or embedded social services. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Selecting the support email link opens your email application.
